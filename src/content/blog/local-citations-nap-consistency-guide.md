Citations are not links. They are votes of confidence in your existence — and inconsistent votes elect your competitor.

## Why cheap citation blasts are toxic

Automated $5 gigs submit your business to abandoned directories with bot-generated data. The result is predictable: mismatched NAP across the web, which reads to Google as an unreliable entity. Rankings don't just stall; filter suppression actively pushes you down. Then there's no owner, no logins, no recovery path.

## The manual standard

Every record reconciled as one canonical entity, submitted only to Tier-1 and Tier-2 registries that move rankings — Apple Business Connect, Bing Places, Yelp, BBB — documented in a spreadsheet you own. Our [Local SEO service](/services/local-seo) runs exactly this playbook across 70+ registries.

## Duplicates: the silent killer

Five addresses for one business force the engine to guess which is canonical. Duplicate suppression — finding every variant and merging or removing it — is unglamorous and decisive. It also protects [Maps 3-pack placement](/blog/google-maps-3-pack-ranking-factors-geo-grid/), since duplicates split your authority across records.

## Citations feed AI answers too

Answer engines corroborate across registries before citing. The same cleanup that lifts Maps rank raises your [AI citation rate](/blog/ai-visibility-local-business-chatgpt-gemini-perplexity/) — one body of work, two surfaces.
