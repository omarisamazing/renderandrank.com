Reviews are the only ranking factor your customers build for you. The businesses winning the [Maps 3-pack](/blog/google-maps-3-pack-ranking-factors-geo-grid/) treat review generation as a pipeline, not a hope.

## The velocity principle

Google weighs recency heavily: ten reviews this month beats five hundred from 2019. Lumina Dental Studio generated 87 authentic five-star reviews in 60 days via post-visit SMS — and moved into the Top 3 while its AI citation rate hit 92%, because answer engines read the same trust signals.

## The SMS template that works

Send within 2 hours of service completion, from a recognized number, with a direct review link — no login mazes. One open question ("What did we fix for you today?") produces specific, keyword-rich reviews without scripting anything.

## Respond to everything

Every review gets a reply with one specific sentence referencing their words. It converts readers, signals engagement, and gives you a public record of service quality that AI summarizers quote.

## Remove the fakes

Competitor spam and review-bombing are removable through GBP redressal with evidence. Document, escalate, and follow up — removals are slow but permanent, and each one returns its stolen rank to someone legitimate.
