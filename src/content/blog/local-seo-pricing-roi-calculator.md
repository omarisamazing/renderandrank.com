Most local SEO pricing is either suspiciously cheap or suspiciously vague. Here's the honest menu — including ours.

## What the tiers actually buy

- **$35 Basic Citation Audit** — complete NAP consistency audit, Tier-1 submission plan, duplicate cleanup checklist.
- **$79 Standard Authority Stack** — full GBP calibration, geo-grid verification, review generation templates.
- **$149 Premium AI & Map Moat** — semantic citation calibration, nested JSON-LD, 30-day telemetry.
- **Retainers $149–$349/mo** — Local Growth Core, Local Lead Machine, Market Dominator. No lock-in contracts; see the full breakdown on [pricing](/pricing).

## How to model the return

Take your grid position to the [ROI calculator](/calculator): it converts missed rank into missed calls and revenue using your ticket size. Apex Climate's 14→186 calls per month is what the top end of that math looks like in production.

## The no-guarantees rule

Rankings depend on Google, competitors, and your market — nobody honest guarantees positions. What can be guaranteed: the audit gets done, submissions are manual and documented, pipelines run, and every claim is verified on geo-grids and [AI checks](/check) you can reproduce. That verifiability is the entire difference between engineering and marketing.
